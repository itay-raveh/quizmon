Quizmon shows you a Pokémon sprite and four names. Pick fast, keep your accuracy up, and find out how much Pokédex trivia still lives in your head.

- Mix Pokémon from Generations I through IX, including regional and special forms.
- Turn every sprite into a **Who’s that Pokémon?** silhouette or choose a random sprite for each question.
- Set the quiz length and enable speedrun mode when the answer reveal starts feeling slow.

## Pokémon data

Quizmon keeps its Pokémon catalog in the repository, then asks [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) for sprites and details during a game. The game is a fan project and has no affiliation with Nintendo, Game Freak, or The Pokémon Company.
