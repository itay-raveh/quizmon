# Privacy and Cookies

_Last updated: September 27, 2026_

Quizmon is a source-available game operated by Itay Raveh. For privacy questions, contact <quizmon@raveh.dev>.

## What We Keep

Guest progress and unfinished rounds stay in your browser. An optional account stores your email address, Trainer name, completed progress, selected preferences, and friend connections so you can sync devices and use leaderboards. Your email and full save are private; other players can see your Trainer name, partner Pokémon, friend code, and ranked results.

If you enable Daily reminders, we store the push subscription and time zone needed to send them. Sign-in uses a session cookie. The game also uses browser storage and cached files to save progress and work offline.

## Analytics and Reports

Sentry counts page views, game starts and completions, and aggregate scores and durations. These metrics do not use a Quizmon visitor or account ID, and we do not set analytics markers in your browser. They count plays, not unique players. Sentry also receives errors and diagnostic traces. Signed-in diagnostics may include your account ID and email. Report a bug sends the details and any screenshot or contact information you choose to submit. We do not record session replays.

Cloudflare hosts and protects Quizmon and provides traffic and security statistics from site requests. Its traffic dashboard estimates unique visitors from IP addresses and can include bots, so this is not a count of unique players. We do not use Cloudflare's browser analytics script. Cloudflare may use [security cookies](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) to protect the site.

## Services, Retention, and Choices

Account identities are stored in PostgreSQL and synced progress in MongoDB. [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) handles hosting and sign-in email; [Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy/) handles analytics and reports. Push services deliver optional reminders. These providers may process information outside your country.

Sentry currently retains events for 30 days. Account data remains until you delete your account from Account. Deletion removes the account and synced progress from the live service and this device. Other devices may keep local copies; backups are not individually purged and expire through normal rotation. Turn off reminders in Quizmon while online to remove their server registration.

You can download your account data from Account. For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, contact <quizmon@raveh.dev>. We cannot recover guest progress stored only in your browser.
