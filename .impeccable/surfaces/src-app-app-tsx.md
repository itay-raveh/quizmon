Quizmon preserves its recognizable wordmark and crisp Pokémon-game landscape. The landing screen keeps its familiar open composition and uses the approved restrained Logo Echo treatment: one large cobalt Daily Challenge button is the primary action, while Settings, Trainer Card, and Start training remain smaller beneath it. The daily control contains its date without an outer card or nested button. After completion, the same footprint becomes a cream share button that shows the score. Once all eight League Badges are earned, one centered cream Quizmon League action appears below the compact control row as a clearly secondary destination.

Quiz states, results, Settings, and the Trainer Card belong to the same small game system without becoming a simulated handheld console. The unlocked Badge Case adds a ruled League gateway with only its name, the exact 15-answer requirement, five square stage markers, and one start or rematch action. The active League question uses a compact I, II, III, IV, C progress rail and the current trial name. A failed run reports the reached trial and retry action. A perfect run reports Champion and links directly to the Hall of Fame Badge Case. These states use mechanical copy only, never slogans or advertising language.

Controls borrow the wordmark's navy outline, cobalt fill, warm yellow lower edge, compact corners, and modest hard shadow. Avoid giant HUD frames, pixel fonts, glass panels, blurred scenery, campaign-style copy, and broad structural changes. Motion is quick and physical through transform and opacity. The first viewport must preserve the approved open composition before responsive adaptation.
